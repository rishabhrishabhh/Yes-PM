The gap

Advice today is either generic or unreachable.

Right now

  • Post and hope. Ask in a Slack or Discord community and wait for someone with the right scar tissue to reply.
  • Book and wait. Free mentors on ADPList are real, but the waitlists are too.
  • Ask an AI coach. Fast, but it's pattern-matching from training data — not a case that actually happened.
  • Read a static library. Good for interview prep. It doesn't know what your situation looks like.
→

With Yes PM

  • Describe the situation. Context, data, constraints — in your own words.
  • Get matched instantly. Retrieval finds analogous cases by problem type, industry, stage, and framework.
  • See what actually happened. Real diagnosis, decision, and outcome — not a best practice.
  • Escalate if you need to. Bring the grounded precedent into a mentor conversation, instead of starting cold.

The loop

One corpus. Three ways in.

Every path — learning, getting unstuck, contributing — feeds the same shared case corpus, so it gets sharper with every PM it helps.

  1. 01

    Describe your situation

    Drop in the context, what's at stake, and what you've already tried — no rigid form, just the real mess.

  2. 02

    Get matched to precedent

    The retrieval engine ranks the closest analogous cases by framework, industry, and company stage.

  3. 03

    Read how it was actually resolved

    Situation, stakes, diagnosis, decision, outcome — the full shape of a real call, not a generic tip.

  4. 04

    Close the loop

    Once your own situation resolves, contribute it back anonymized — and strengthen the next match for someone else.
